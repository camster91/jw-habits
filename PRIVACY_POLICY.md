# Faithful Days privacy policy

Effective: 2026-10-28. Developer: Cameron Ashley (Ashbi Design).
The published policy is the authoritative text:
https://camster91.github.io/jw-habits/privacy/ (source: `site/privacy/index.html`).
Support: https://camster91.github.io/jw-habits/support/

In short, nothing is collected and nothing is shared.

- Routines, reading history, plans, notes, meeting preparation, assignments,
  dates, settings and badges are stored on your device only. There are no
  accounts, advertising or analytics, and no third-party software that collects
  data.
- A backup is a file you choose to export from Settings. Backups are not
  encrypted; keep them private. Importing replaces the data on that device
  after you confirm. Your phone's own backup service may also include app data.
- Reminders are scheduled locally with your permission, not sent from a server.
- Once a day the app requests jw.org's public What's New RSS feed and reads only
  each item's identifier and publication date. The request carries no personal
  data from the app, though jw.org's servers see ordinary network information
  such as an IP address. It is on by default and can be switched off in
  Settings, after which the app makes no network requests of its own.
- Opening a link leaves the app; the destination's privacy practices apply.
- Uninstalling removes the data the app holds on the device. Exported backup
  files are separate.
- The app is not directed at children under 13.

Faithful Days is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
sponsored by Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society or jw.org, and contains no
content from jw.org.
