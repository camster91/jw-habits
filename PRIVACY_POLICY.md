# Privacy Policy for JW Progress

**Last updated: February 10, 2026**

## Introduction

Ashbi Design ("we", "our", or "us") built the JW Progress app as a free app. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for anyone who uses our app.

## Information Collection and Use

JW Progress does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. All data is stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage.

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

- Daily reading progress and completion status
- - Streak tracking data
  - - User preferences and settings
    - - Notification preferences
     
      - This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.
     
      - ## Third-Party Services
     
      - The app provides links to JW Library and jw.org, which are third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external services. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you visit.
     
      - ## Cookies
     
      - JW Progress does not use cookies or any tracking technologies.
     
      - ## Security
     
      - We value your trust. Since all data is stored locally on your device, your information is as secure as your device itself. No data is transmitted over the internet by our app.
     
      - ## Children's Privacy
     
      - Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, this app is safe for users of all ages.
     
      - ## Changes to This Privacy Policy
     
      - We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
     
      - ## Contact Us
     
      - If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at cameron@ashbi.ca.
     
      - ## Disclaimer
     
      - JW Progress is an unofficial application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Jehovah's Witnesses or the Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society of Pennsylvania.
