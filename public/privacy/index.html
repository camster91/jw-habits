Faithful Days

Privacy policy

Updated 7 October 2026.

Faithful Days keeps your routines, reading history, plans, notes, settings and badges on your device. There are no app accounts, advertising or third-party analytics. Your spiritual activity is not uploaded to Ashbi Design.

Storage and your choices

The web app uses browser storage; the native app also uses on-device app storage. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the app can remove your history. Export a backup from Settings → Backup before changing devices. Backups are not encrypted: keep them private. Importing a backup replaces the current app data after confirmation. Your device or operating system may include app data in its own backup service.

Network connections

Loading the web app sends normal web requests to its hosting service. The server receives information such as your IP address, request path and browser headers, and may keep operational access logs. These requests do not include your routines, plans or notes.

On native devices, the optional What’s New feature checks the public jw.org feed at most daily. The receiving server gets ordinary network information such as your IP address. We do not attach your activity, notes, account details or a persistent app identifier. Switch this feature off in Settings if you do not want these requests.

Opening a link leaves Faithful Days and opens your browser or another app, such as JW Library. That destination’s privacy practices apply.

Reminders, widgets and diagnostics

Reminders are scheduled on your device with your permission. Widgets use a local snapshot and local check-in queue. Diagnostic history contains error times and categories only, stays on-device, is limited to 20 entries and expires after seven days when the app next opens and can be cleared in Settings → About. It is not sent to us automatically.

Sharing and support

Share cards are generated on your device only after you tap Share card. Cards exclude step notes and links. On a native device you choose the destination in the share sheet; on web you download a PNG. Backup files may contain private information. If you email support, the information you choose to send is received through our email provider. Do not send passwords, signing keys or private study notes.
